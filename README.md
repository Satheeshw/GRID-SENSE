# GRID-SENSE

    GridSense – AI-Powered Smart Energy Monitoring & Safety System is an IoT-integrated solution developed to provide intelligent, real-time monitoring of electrical energy consumption and improve appliance safety in three-phase power systems. Conventional energy monitoring systems primarily provide overall consumption data, offering limited information about individual appliance usage and abnormal electrical behaviour. GridSense addresses this gap by continuously monitoring voltage, current, power and harmonic parameters from a three-phase supply and processing the collected data using a Raspberry Pi.
    The key innovation of GridSense is the integration of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning with electrical monitoring. An XGBoost-based Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) model analyses electrical consumption patterns to identify active appliances without requiring individual monitoring for every appliance. An Isolation Forest model detects abnormal electrical patterns associated with potentially fake, substandard or unsafe appliances.
    The processed data and AI-generated insights are transmitted to the Firebase Realtime Database and presented through cloud-based dashboards designed for consumers and government authorities. The system provides appliance-level energy insights, anomaly detection and safety alerts, supporting better energy management and informed decision-making.
    By combining IoT, AI, real-time energy monitoring, anomaly detection and cloud technologies into a single affordable platform, GridSense aims to improve energy efficiency, enhance electrical safety, increase transparency and contribute to the reduction of energy losses. The developed prototype is being refined toward practical real-world deployment.
